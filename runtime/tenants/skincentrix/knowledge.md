# Skincentrix

Mississauga's leading medi-clinic. Beauty specialists and nurses.

## Location and contact
2285 Britannia Rd W, Mississauga, ON L5M 2G6. Phone 905-703-7546 (905-703-SKIN). Email info@skincentrix.com. Website skincentrix.com.

## Booking
Clients book online at skincentrix.janeapp.com. New clients usually start with a free virtual consultation (phone, video, FaceTime or WhatsApp).

## Offers and payment options
- $50 first-visit credit for new clients.
- Multi-session packages: 3-session and 8-session series.
- SkinC Club memberships.
- Financing is available; the team explains options.
- Gift cards are available.
- Retail skincare is sold in clinic and online, including the plated line.

## Policies
Cancellation and rescheduling requests are handled by the team, who confirm by phone or email. (Exact cancellation terms: confirm with the clinic before go-live.)

## Training courses
Skincentrix runs training courses for practitioners. Those enquiries go to a different team member.

## What the assistant does not do
No medical advice, no advice on reactions or symptoms after a treatment, no payment details, no clinical records.
