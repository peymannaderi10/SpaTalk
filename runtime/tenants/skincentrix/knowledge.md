# Skincentrix

Mississauga's leading medi-clinic and skin wellness destination, founded ten years ago by Sabah Shaikh and co-run by Sabah and Umer. Over 100,000 clients treated. Treatments are personalised and multi-step, often fifteen or more layered steps in one session, rather than one-size-fits-all. Beauty specialists, medical aestheticians and registered nurses on the team.
